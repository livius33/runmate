<objective>
## Goal
The page changes the plan as a whole. Four areas on a wide grid; every lever shows the state of its data, a preview
old against new with reasons, and applies only after a confirmation. One history with undo for everything.

## Purpose
Maximum freedom for the runner as long as the data carries it, easy to use, and every change explained.
</objective>

<acceptance_criteria>
AC-1  Ziel und Rennen: goal time, race date, method. Each field shows the current value and the data check; "Vorschau"
      opens the comparison; "Übernehmen" applies it.
AC-2  Alltag: training days, long-run day, caps (the profile form that exists), with the sentence that a live plan
      follows only after a rebuild, and the same preview.
AC-3  Unterbrechung: Krank/Pause marking (exists) on this page too; from 14 days the rebuild offer with the return base.
AC-4  Vorschläge und Verlauf: the existing suggestions for a period plus one history of rebuilds and taken-over
      suggestions, each with undo.
AC-5  Without Plus the preview works and "Übernehmen" shows the calm upsell; nothing else on the page is locked.
AC-6  Without a live generated block the page says what is missing and links to `/plan/neu`.
AC-7  Wide grid on desktop (no narrow column of stacked cards), one column on a phone without horizontal scroll.
AC-8  Checked in the browser at 390 px and 1440 px (08-03).
</acceptance_criteria>
