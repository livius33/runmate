<objective>
## Goal
Runmate can send the three mails it promises (waitlist confirmation, cancellation confirmation, notice before an
idle account is deleted), and the jobs that exist in code but are not scheduled run.

## Purpose
`lib/waitlist/index.ts` logs instead of sending in production: whoever joins the waitlist waits for a mail that
never comes. `/api/cron/expired-accounts` and `/api/cron/demo-reset` are not in `vercel.json`, so what the privacy
page says about deletion does not happen.
</objective>

<context>
## Decisions Livius has to make (checkpoint, task 1)

1. Mail provider and sender address (needs DNS records for runmate.dev: SPF, DKIM, DMARC). Or: no waitlist at all
   and open sign-up from day one, which removes the first mail.
2. Deleting expired accounts on a schedule: yes, and from when. It deletes stored user data, so it is not switched
   on without an explicit yes.
3. From 12-03: store link tokens as hashes (existing calendar and Apple Health links must be re-created once),
   keep the Garmin consumer pair in an env var, key ids for `ENCRYPTION_KEY`.
</context>

<acceptance_criteria>
AC-1  A mailer behind the existing interface sends through the chosen provider; without its key nothing is sent
      and the waitlist form says so instead of promising a mail.
AC-2  Waitlist: one confirmation per address in 15 minutes, a per-IP limit, and a bot check on the form.
AC-3  Every cancellation through the public form (12-05) sends a mail to the account's address with the end date
      and the take-back link.
AC-4  The scheduled jobs agreed in the checkpoint are in `vercel.json`; each has a dry-run mode whose output Livius
      has seen before the first real run.
AC-5  `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npx vitest run` and `npm run build` are green.
</acceptance_criteria>

<tasks>
1. Checkpoint with Livius: the decisions above.
2. Mailer and templates (German, plain text plus simple HTML).
3. Waitlist limits and bot check.
4. Cancellation mail.
5. Schedules, dry run, first run.
6. Whatever of decision 3 was chosen.
</tasks>

<boundaries>

- No deletion job runs before Livius has seen its dry run.
</boundaries>
