<objective>
## Goal
Close what the security review of 2026-11-21 found before strangers can sign up, and before billing is switched on.

## Purpose
Livius asked for login, onboarding, payment and the security setup to be checked, and whether any key or token is
exposed. Nothing is exposed and tenant isolation holds; the review still found gaps that matter once the hosted
instance is public. No instance is live yet and billing is off, so nothing here is exploitable today.
</objective>

<context>
## What was checked

- Secrets: gitleaks over the full history of every ref, a pattern scan over every git object, and a search for the
  exact values of the local env files (runmate and dashboard) in every object. PR texts, review comments, Actions
  settings, repository secrets, deploy keys, hooks, releases. Result: nothing. Test fakes only
  (`pdl_ntfset_test_secret`, `postgres://runmate:runmate@db`). Commit e-mails are GitHub noreply addresses.
- Two independent code reviews: (1) authentication, authorisation, tenant isolation, tokens, import, headers;
  (2) Paddle, webhooks, entitlements, encryption, secrets in code, onboarding path, DEPLOY.md against the code.
- Dependencies: Dependabot alerts and `npm audit --omit=dev`.

## What holds

Every handler establishes the user itself (`withUser`), every query is scoped (`own()`/`mine()`, enforced by
`tests/db/scoping.test.ts`), 58 route files checked, no IDOR. Webhooks verify signatures on the raw body and refuse
everything without their secret. The checkout carries a server-signed user id. Every Plus feature is enforced on the
server. AES-256-GCM with a fresh IV; credentials are left out of the export. Import has size, entry and depth caps
and no entity expansion. No user-supplied URL is fetched, no open redirect, no HTML sink with user data.

## Findings

| # | Severity | Finding | Matters from |
|---|----------|---------|--------------|
| 1 | high | `next` 15.5.15 has open advisories: middleware bypass (the `ALLOWED_EMAILS` gate lives only in the middleware), image-optimiser RCE (self-hosters), DoS. Also axios, follow-redirects, drizzle-orm, postcss, sharp, fast-xml-parser | stage A |
| 2 | high | The landing CTA links to `/sign-up` once billing is on; the route does not exist | stage B |
| 3 | medium | `POST /api/billing/cancel-request` cancels any subscriber's plan by e-mail: no proof of identity, no confirmation, no limit | stage B |
| 4 | medium | Garmin connect has no attempt limit: a free account can test Garmin passwords from the server's address, and Garmin may block that address for everybody | stage A |
| 5 | medium | Health consent is enforced on the intake routes only; profile (heart rate, weight), run notes and pain, RPE and week feedback are stored without it | stage A |
| 6 | medium | No rate limits at all (import, export, sync, waitlist, cancel form) | stage A |
| 7 | medium | Two subscriptions for one account are possible (buy buttons stay until the webhook arrives; events are applied whatever their subscription id); cancel and delete stop only the stored one | stage B |
| 8 | medium | A failed Paddle cancel during account deletion is logged without any id; the row is deleted, the customer keeps paying | stage B |
| 9 | medium | Checkout opens although `PADDLE_WEBHOOK_SECRET` is missing; nothing ever reconciles with Paddle | stage B |
| 10 | low | No security headers (no frame-ancestors, nosniff, Referrer-Policy, CSP); `X-Powered-By` on; no `authorizedParties` | stage A |
| 11 | low | Trial restarts with a new account; go-live race lets a Free account take two plans live | stage B |
| 12 | low | Link tokens stored in plaintext; cron secret compared with `!==`; ICS escaping misses `;` and lone `\r` | any |
| 13 | low | Garmin OAuth consumer pair is fetched at runtime from a third-party bucket | any |
| 14 | low | GCM tag length not pinned on decrypt; no key id, so rotating `ENCRYPTION_KEY` disconnects every Garmin login | any |
| 15 | note | `/api/cron/expired-accounts` and `/api/cron/demo-reset` are not scheduled; the waitlist mailer sends nothing | stage A |

<acceptance_criteria>
