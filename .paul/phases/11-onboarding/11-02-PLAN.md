<objective>
## Goal
The week review that opens on Monday fits on one phone screen without scrolling: the week in three figures, the one
thing to take along, the rating. Everything else is one tap away.

## Purpose
Livius: "Die Feature Card finde ich gut, aber immer noch zu groß. Die muss einfach kurz, knapp, knackig sein."
Read as the week review overlay (to confirm with Livius). The overlay exists to collect two ratings; today it shows
six figures, four lists and a form first (800 px on a desktop, 1030 px of content on a phone).
</objective>

<context>
Measured before (first test user, KW 46, plan moved three weeks back in the test database so a finished week exists):
1440 px: modal 832 × 799 px. 390 px: sheet 776 px high with 1029 px of content, the rating below the fold.
Also found: the week target is printed with a decimal point ("45/47.5 km").
</context>

<acceptance_criteria>
AC-1  First view: week strip, three figures (volume against target, runs against planned, key sessions run against
      planned), the focus line, the two ratings, "Später" and "Bewertung speichern". Nothing else.
AC-2  At 390 × 844 px the first view needs no scrolling; on a desktop the modal is at most 34 rem wide and clearly
      lower than before (target under 560 px).
AC-3  "Details" opens what the long version had: easy share, form, fitness, load, feeling of the runs, key sessions
      with their verdict, plan changes, notable runs, next week. Empty lists are left out instead of saying that
      nothing happened.
AC-4  The note is one tap away ("Notiz hinzufügen"); a stored note is shown open.
AC-5  Rating, "Später", Esc, focus trap and the stored-at line behave as before; the stored shape does not change.
AC-6  German decimal comma for the week target, in the overlay, the card and the focus text.
AC-7  The three figures come from one pure function with tests (singular forms, a week without key sessions, a moved
      key session is not counted as planned).
AC-8  Checked in the browser at 390 px and 1440 px, closed and with details open, no console errors.
</acceptance_criteria>
