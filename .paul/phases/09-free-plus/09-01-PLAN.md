<objective>
The hosted app has two states a runner can be in: Free and Plus. Free is a real product, not a locked account;
Plus costs 9 €/month or 79 €/year. A new account gets 30 days of Plus without a card and then continues as Free.
Self-hosting stays complete and free.
</objective>

<context>
Decided by Livius on 2026-11-17 (see .paul/STATE.md). Today the code sells Basic (6 €) and Plus (10 €) and locks an
account after the trial ("expired": no sync, no plan). That lock goes away.

Free: data sources and import, analysis, insights, one live plan edited by hand, the engine's changes as suggestions
to take over, export, delete.
Plus: the engine applies its changes by itself, whole-plan rebuild on Plan anpassen (goal, date, interruption),
several plans and B-races, own method, race-day tools, sleep and HRV feeding the plan.
</context>

<decisions>
- `PlanState` = self-host | trial | free | plus | past_due. `expired` is removed; everything that was expired is free.
- `Tier` = 'plus' only. A stored `tier: 'basic'` (none exist in production) counts as free.
- Two new features: `rebuild` (used by Phase 10) and `recoveryInPlan` (night flags join the engine's fitness only
  with it; the recovery card and the analysis stay free).
- Free = sync, plan, export, delete. Plus = autoAdapt, rebuild, multiPlan, ownMethod, raceDay, recoveryInPlan.
- past_due keeps Plus until Paddle cancels.
- Retention is unchanged in substance: a free account without a visit for 12 months gets the reminder and is deleted
  30 days later. Paying, trialing and past_due accounts are never touched. The cron path stays.
- Paddle: two prices (`PADDLE_PRICE_PLUS_MONTH`, `PADDLE_PRICE_PLUS_YEAR`). No schema change.
- Texts: calm, no pressure, no permanent banner for Free. Label "Kostenlos" / "Free".
</decisions>

<acceptance_criteria>
AC-1  Given billing on and a trial that ended, when entitlements are read, then state is `free`, sync and plan are
      allowed, maxLivePlans is 1 and every Plus feature is off.
AC-2  Given a free account with one running plan, when a second plan goes live, then 402 `plus-required` with a
      German text that names Plus and says nothing about "abgelaufen".
AC-3  Given a free account, when the engine runs, then its plan changes come back as suggestions and night flags do
      not enter the fitness; given Plus, trial or self-host, they do.
AC-4  Given any state, export and delete are allowed.
AC-5  `/api/billing` offers exactly the configured Plus prices; the settings card shows one Plus offer with
      "9 € / Monat" and "79 € / Jahr" and what Free includes.
AC-6  The landing page shows Self-host 0 €, Free 0 € and Plus 9 € / 79 € in German and English; the structured data
      matches.
AC-7  AGB and the cancellation page name only Plus as the paid plan and describe the fall back to Free.
AC-8  No "Basic", "6 €", "59 €", "10 €" or "99 €" is left in app/, components/, lib/, README.md, .env.example and
      docs/LAUNCH-PLAN.md (docs/phases/ is history and stays).
AC-9  `npx tsc --noEmit` and `npm test` are green.
</acceptance_criteria>
